Best Tax Write-Offs for Delivery Drivers in 2026

Educational guide · not tax advice. Confirm current figures at IRS.gov.

A write-off is a cost the tax code lets you subtract. It is not a refund of the whole expense, and it is not a list of things that “usually” save a set number of dollars. For a DoorDash, Uber Eats, or other 1099 delivery driver, the large items are car costs and the other ordinary costs of taking orders. Confirm each one against your own receipts.

The car: pick one method

IRS Topic 510 gives you two ways to figure car expenses.

  • Standard mileage. Business miles times the IRS rate. For 2026 that is 72.5 cents through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1. You must be allowed to use the rate (see Publication 463). For a car you own, you choose it in the first year the car is available for the business. Later years you may switch to actual expenses. For a leased car, choosing the rate means keeping it for the whole lease, including renewals.
  • Actual expenses. The business percentage of gas, oil, repairs, tires, insurance, registration, and depreciation or lease payments. Business percentage is business miles divided by all miles for the year.

You do not deduct the standard rate and those actual operating costs for the same car in the same year. Publication 463 is explicit about that.

Items you can still deduct with the standard rate

Business parking and tolls are separate. Parking at your regular place of work is a commuting cost and is not deductible. Publication 463 also says a self-employed person can deduct the business percentage of interest on a car loan on Schedule C even when using the standard mileage rate, and the business part of state and local personal property tax on the vehicle. Do not deduct the same interest twice if you also qualify for the separate passenger-vehicle loan interest deduction on Schedule 1-A.

Other costs, only the business part

Insulated bags, a phone mount used for offers, and the business percentage of a phone plan can be ordinary costs of the work. The personal percentage is not a business expense. Keep the receipt and a note of how you split personal use. There is no IRS table that says a hot bag is worth a particular deduction.

What this does to the tax

Allowable expenses lower net profit. Net profit is what Schedule SE starts from. Estimate that tax with the self-employment tax calculator after you have a real profit figure, and price a mileage deduction with the mileage calculator.

Common questions

What is the biggest write-off for a delivery driver?

For many contractors it is the car, either business miles times the IRS rate or actual expenses times the business-use percentage. Which one is larger depends on your miles, your car, and your receipts. Figure both before you choose, if you are allowed to use both.

Can I deduct my whole phone bill?

Only the part you can show was for the delivery work. Personal use of the same phone is not a business expense.

Do write-offs include the IRS mileage rate and gas?

Not for the same vehicle in the same year. The standard mileage rate replaces gas, insurance, repairs, and depreciation. Tolls and business parking are extra.
