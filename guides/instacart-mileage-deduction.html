Instacart Mileage Deduction: Which Miles Count

Educational guide · not tax advice. Confirm current figures at IRS.gov.

This page is for shoppers who drive as independent contractors and file a Schedule C. If Instacart treats you as an employee, employee vehicle expenses are a different set of rules. Check your papers before you deduct anything.

Two rates in 2026

The business standard mileage rate is 72.5 cents per mile for miles driven January 1 through June 30, 2026, and 76 cents per mile for miles driven July 1 through December 31, 2026. Source: the IRS standard mileage rates. Split the year in your log. One blended rate for all 12 months will not match the IRS tables.

Example, not a guess about a typical shopper: 400 business miles before July 1 are 400 × $0.725 = $290. Four hundred business miles on or after July 1 are 400 × $0.76 = $304. Business parking and tolls are added on top. They are not inside the cents-per-mile rate.

Miles between orders are not the same as the drive from home

Publication 463 says transportation between your home and your main or regular place of work is personal commuting. If you have no regular office and you do not have an office in your home, the publication treats the first business stop inside your metropolitan area as the office: the drive from home to that first stop, and the drive from the last stop back home, are nondeductible commuting. Miles from one store or customer to the next can be deductible.

There is an exception when your home is your principal place of business and you drive between home and another work location in the same business. Read the commuting chapter of Publication 463 before you claim every mile from the moment you leave the driveway. A lot of delivery advice skips that rule.

What to write down

IRS Topic 510 and Publication 463 expect a record of the date, the miles for each business use, the destination, the business purpose, and the total miles for the year. A week-later guess is a weak record. Use the mileage deduction calculator only after the log exists, and read what the IRS expects in the log.

Common questions

Can I deduct the drive from home to the first Instacart store?

Publication 463 treats the trip from home to the first business stop, and from the last stop home, as commuting when you have no qualifying home office. Miles between stores and customers are the miles the publication allows you to deduct.

What is the Instacart mileage rate for 2026?

There is no Instacart rate. The IRS business rate is 72.5 cents per mile through June 30, 2026, and 76 cents per mile after that.

Can I deduct gas and the standard mileage rate?

No. Publication 463 says that if you use the standard mileage rate you cannot also deduct gas, oil, insurance, registration, lease payments, maintenance, repairs, or depreciation for that car.
