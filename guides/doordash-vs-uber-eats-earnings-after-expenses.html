DoorDash vs Uber Eats Earnings After Expenses

Educational guide · not tax advice. Confirm current figures at IRS.gov.

DoorDash and Uber Eats do not pay a wage. Each offer is a pile of gross pay, and the apps do not subtract the gas you burned or the minutes you waited. A higher payout on one app is not a higher hourly rate until you count the same costs on both.

Use one worksheet for both apps

For each shift, write down platform pay, tips, fees or cash adjustments, miles driven, fuel or your miles-per-gallon and the price you paid, and all hours you were online, including slow time. Then:

  1. Gross = pay + tips.
  2. Fuel = miles ÷ MPG × price per gallon, if you are measuring cash spent.
  3. Cash left = gross − fees − fuel − other cash costs.
  4. Cash per hour = cash left ÷ online hours.
  5. Cash per mile = cash left ÷ miles.

Compare those last two numbers, not the offer screen. A $12 DoorDash order that took 40 minutes and 11 miles can lose to an $8 Uber Eats order that took 15 minutes and 3 miles.

Do not treat a mileage deduction as cash

The IRS business standard mileage rate for 2026 is 72.5 cents per mile from January 1 through June 30 and 76 cents from July 1 through December 31. See the IRS standard mileage rates. That rate is a tax deduction if you choose the standard mileage method. It is not money the app paid you, and it is not the same as the fuel that left your bank account. Keep the cash comparison and the tax deduction in separate columns or you will count the car twice.

What this comparison cannot tell you

It cannot tell you which company pays more in your city. Pay, tips, and deadhead miles change by hour and by zone. Run your own shifts through the gig driver profit calculator, then set aside tax with the self-employment tax calculator.

Common questions

Does DoorDash pay more than Uber Eats after expenses?

Neither company publishes a rate that is your profit. Compare your own pay, tips, fees, miles, fuel, and online hours. A larger offer can be worse per hour.

Should I subtract the IRS mileage rate from a DoorDash shift?

Only when you are estimating a tax deduction. For cash left in your pocket, subtract fuel and other money you spent. The 2026 business rates are 72.5 cents and then 76 cents per mile, and they are not a reimbursement.

Which hours count?

Count every hour the app was on, including waiting. Leaving wait time out inflates the hourly number the same way on both apps.
