How Much Do Gig Drivers Really Make After Expenses?

Educational guide · not tax advice

The number in your delivery app is gross pay. Your real income is what's left after fuel, vehicle wear, fees, and taxes — and after counting all the hours you were online, including waiting.

The formula

Net profit = pay + tips − fees − fuel − wear − other costs. Fuel = miles ÷ MPG × gas price. Per hour = net ÷ online hours.

Worked example

$150 pay + $50 tips, 100 miles at 25 MPG and $4 gas ($16 fuel), $0.10/mile wear ($10): net $174 over 8 hours = $21.75/hour, $1.74/mile — before taxes.

Don't forget taxes

As a 1099 contractor you owe self-employment tax (15.3% on 92.35% of net profit) once net earnings reach $400, per the IRS. Use our self-employment tax calculator.
