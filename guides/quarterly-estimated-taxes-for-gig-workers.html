Quarterly Estimated Taxes for Gig Workers, Explained

Educational guide · not tax advice

No employer withholds tax from 1099 income, so the IRS generally expects you to pay during the year using Form 1040-ES if you expect to owe $1,000 or more.

Usual due dates

Typically mid-April, mid-June, mid-September and mid-January — confirm the exact dates for the year at IRS Form 1040-ES.

How much?

Estimate self-employment tax + income tax on profit, then divide by four. Our calculator does it: $30,000 profit at 12% ≈ $1,896 per quarter.
