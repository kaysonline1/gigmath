How to Set Your Freelance Hourly Rate (Without Guessing)

Educational guide · not tax advice

New freelancers often copy their old hourly wage. That ignores taxes, unpaid admin time, and expenses an employer used to cover.

The method

  1. Pick your take-home goal.
  2. Add yearly business expenses (software, equipment, insurance).
  3. Gross up for taxes: (income + expenses) ÷ (1 − tax rate).
  4. Divide by realistic billable hours (often 20–30/week).

Example

$60,000 goal + $6,000 expenses at a 25% set-aside = $88,000 needed. 25 hrs × 46 weeks = 1,150 hours → about $76.52/hour.
