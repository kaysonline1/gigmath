Mileage Deduction for Gig Workers: 2026 Guide

Educational guide · not tax advice

If you drive for work as an independent contractor, the standard mileage deduction is often the largest write-off you have.

Standard mileage vs. actual expenses

You can deduct business miles × the IRS standard rate (plus business tolls and parking), or actual vehicle expenses. Our calculator defaults to $0.725/mile for 2026; always check the current rate at irs.gov/standard-mileage-rates.

Keep a log

The IRS expects a record of date, miles, destination and business purpose. Our free printable log covers all of it.

Example

10,000 business miles × $0.725 + $50 tolls = $7,300 deduction. At a 25% combined rate that's roughly $1,825 less tax. Try the calculator.
