Mileage Deduction for Gig Workers: 2026 Guide

Educational guide · not tax advice. Confirm current figures at IRS.gov.

If you drive for work as an independent contractor, the standard mileage deduction is often the largest write-off you have.

Standard mileage vs. actual expenses

You can deduct business miles × the IRS standard rate (plus business tolls and parking), or actual vehicle expenses for that car. For 2026 the IRS business rate is 72.5 cents per mile from January 1 through June 30 (Notice 2026-10) and 76 cents per mile from July 1 through December 31 (Announcement 2026-11). Check irs.gov/standard-mileage-rates before you file.

Keep a log

The IRS expects a record of date, miles, destination and business purpose. Our free printable log covers all of it.

Example

1,000 business miles in the first half of 2026 × $0.725 = $725. The same 1,000 miles in the second half × $0.76 = $760. Tolls and parking for those trips are added on top. Try the calculator with the rate for the miles you are counting.
