How Much to Save for Taxes as a DoorDash Driver

Educational guide · not tax advice. Confirm current figures at IRS.gov.

DoorDash does not withhold federal income tax or Social Security and Medicare tax from a 1099 driver. If you are an independent contractor, you figure that tax yourself. There is no IRS percentage that says “every Dasher should save 30 percent.” The amount depends on your net profit, any W-2 wages, and your income-tax situation.

Start with net profit, not the weekly deposit

Self-employment tax uses net earnings from the business: gross pay and tips minus the ordinary and necessary expenses you are allowed to deduct. IRS Topic 554 says you usually must pay self-employment tax if those net earnings are $400 or more. On Schedule SE, net profit is multiplied by 92.35 percent (0.9235). If that result is under $400, you generally stop and owe no self-employment tax.

The 15.3 percent is two taxes

The IRS self-employment tax page rate is 15.3 percent of net earnings after the 92.35 percent step: 12.4 percent for Social Security and 2.9 percent for Medicare. For 2026, the Social Security portion applies only up to $184,500 of combined wages and self-employment earnings (the figure printed on the 2026 Schedule SE). Medicare has no wage cap. You also deduct half of the self-employment tax when you figure income tax. That half does not reduce the self-employment tax itself.

A labeled example, not a typical Dasher

Suppose Schedule C net profit for the year is $20,000 and you have no W-2 wages. This is an illustration, not an average.

  • Net earnings = $20,000 × 0.9235 = $18,470.
  • Social Security = $18,470 × 0.124 = $2,290.28.
  • Medicare = $18,470 × 0.029 = $535.63.
  • Self-employment tax = $2,825.91.
  • Half of that, $1,412.96, is the income-tax deduction.

Income tax is extra. It depends on filing status, the standard or itemized deduction, credits, and other income. Type your own income-tax estimate into the self-employment tax calculator. Dividing the total by four is only a rough quarterly set-aside, not the annualized method in Publication 505.

An extra 0.9 percent Additional Medicare Tax can apply above the filing-status thresholds in IRS Topic 560 ($200,000 single or head of household, $250,000 married filing jointly, $125,000 married filing separately). The calculator on this site does not add that tax.

Common questions

Is 25 or 30 percent the official DoorDash tax rate?

No. The IRS self-employment tax is 15.3 percent of net earnings after multiplying profit by 92.35 percent, and only after net earnings reach $400. Income tax is separate and is not a single gig-driver rate.

Do I owe tax if DoorDash never sent a form?

You report the income even when no information return arrives. A missing form does not erase the earnings.

Does the mileage deduction lower self-employment tax?

If you use the standard mileage rate, those business miles reduce net profit. A lower net profit means a lower self-employment tax. You cannot also deduct the same gas, insurance, and depreciation.
