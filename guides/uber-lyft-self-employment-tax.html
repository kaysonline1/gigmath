Uber and Lyft Self-Employment Tax Explained

Educational guide · not tax advice. Confirm current figures at IRS.gov.

Uber and Lyft do not pay the employer half of Social Security and Medicare for an independent-contractor driver. You pay both halves through self-employment tax, on top of income tax. Age does not turn it off. IRS Topic 554 says the tax can apply even if you already receive Social Security benefits.

Who files Schedule SE

You use Schedule SE if you are subject to the tax. The usual trigger is net earnings from self-employment of $400 or more. Net earnings start with Schedule C profit. Schedule SE multiplies that profit by 92.35 percent. If the result is under $400, the form tells you to stop, with a narrow exception for church employee income that does not apply to rideshare.

2026 rates and the wage cap

On the amount that remains:

  • 12.4 percent is Social Security, only until combined Social Security wages and self-employment earnings hit the cap. The 2026 Schedule SE prints that cap as $184,500.
  • 2.9 percent is Medicare, with no cap.
  • Together those two are the familiar 15.3 percent.

If a W-2 job already had Social Security tax withheld on wages up to the cap, you do not pay the 12.4 percent again on self-employment earnings above what is left of the cap. You still pay the 2.9 percent Medicare portion on net earnings.

IRS Topic 560 adds 0.9 percent Additional Medicare Tax when wages and self-employment income are above $250,000 married filing jointly, $125,000 married filing separately, or $200,000 for single, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse. That tax is computed on Form 8959. It is not inside the 15.3 percent.

The deduction people forget

You deduct half of the self-employment tax as an adjustment to income. That lowers income tax. It does not lower the self-employment tax you just calculated.

Put your net profit and any W-2 Social Security wages into the self-employment tax calculator. The miles that got you to that profit belong in the standard mileage versus actual expenses choice, not in a second subtraction after you have already reduced profit.

Common questions

Do Uber drivers pay self-employment tax on gross fares?

No. The tax is on net earnings. Subtract the car method you are allowed to use, and your other deductible costs, before the 92.35 percent step.

What is the 2026 Social Security wage base for rideshare tax?

The 2026 Schedule SE uses $184,500 as the maximum combined wages and self-employment earnings subject to the Social Security portion.

Does a W-2 job cancel Uber self-employment tax?

It can use up the Social Security cap, so the 12.4 percent may not apply to your driving earnings. The 2.9 percent Medicare portion still applies to net earnings of $400 or more.
