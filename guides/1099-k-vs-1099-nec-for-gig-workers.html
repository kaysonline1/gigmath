1099-K vs 1099-NEC for Gig Workers

Educational guide · not tax advice. Confirm current figures at IRS.gov.

A form is a copy of what a company told the IRS. It is not a bill, and it is not a complete list of what you earned. Gig drivers and freelancers see two different forms for two different kinds of payments.

Form 1099-K

The IRS 1099-K guide covers payments settled by a payment card or by a third-party settlement organization such as a payment app or online marketplace. For third-party network transactions, the IRS instructions say a company must report when payments to you for goods or services exceed $20,000 and the number of those transactions exceeds 200. Payment-card transactions do not have that de minimis threshold: the IRS FAQ says there is no dollar floor for payment-card payments.

A company may still send a 1099-K below the third-party threshold. Your state may have a lower threshold. Box 1a is a gross amount. It is not reduced for fees, refunds, or credits, so it can be larger than the cash that reached your bank.

Form 1099-NEC

Form 1099-NEC is for nonemployee compensation paid directly in a trade or business. The IRS page on information returns says the reporting threshold is $600 for payments made before 2026 and $2,000 for payments made in 2026. That is the payer’s filing threshold. It is not a rule that you omit income under $2,000. Backup withholding can require a form even when the payment is smaller.

What you report

Report all taxable income whether or not a form showed up, and do not report the same dollars twice if a platform’s payments appear on more than one form. Fees taken before you were paid are not automatically deductible just because the 1099-K is gross; you deduct them when they are ordinary and necessary business expenses you can substantiate. None of this depends on which logo is on the app. Platforms change vendors and form types. Read the form you received.

After you know gross income and the expenses that belong against it, estimate the tax with the self-employment tax calculator.

Common questions

If my gig app pays me $10,000, will I get a 1099-K?

Not necessarily from a third-party settlement organization. The federal reporting rule for those payments is over $20,000 and more than 200 transactions. The company may send a form anyway, and payment-card payments have no dollar threshold. You still report the income.

Does the 2026 1099-NEC $2,000 threshold mean I do not owe tax under that?

No. That figure is when the payer must file the form for 2026 payments. Your return includes taxable income below the filing threshold for the form.

The 1099-K is higher than my deposits. Which number do I use?

Box 1a is gross. Fees, refunds, and credits may still be inside it. Reconcile the form to your own records instead of picking the smaller number with no paperwork.
