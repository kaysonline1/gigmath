# Niche research — GigMath (researched 2026-10-17)
**Niche picked:** free money calculators for US gig drivers (DoorDash/Uber/Instacart) and 1099 freelancers: profit per hour, mileage deduction, self-employment/quarterly tax, freelance rate, invoices.

## Why
- **There's search demand:** several sites are built just to rank for these searches, which shows people look for them: tools.tnaado.ca/us/gig-driver-profit-per-hour-calculator, keencalc.com/finance/gig-earnings-calculator, gigtakehome.com, and Jackson Hewitt's 2026 self-employment tax calculator (jacksonhewitt.com/tax-tools/...). I did NOT measure keyword volume; no free volume tool was used. Check it with Google Search Console after launch.
- **Competition looks beatable:** many results on page 1 are small, single-purpose sites (tnaado, keencalc, gigtakehome). Big brands (Jackson Hewitt) cover tax but not gig profit per hour.
- **Free-to-join monetization (none applied for, none approved):**
  - FreshBooks affiliate on PartnerStack, free to apply: $10 per qualified trial, up to $200 per paid plan (freshbooks.com/affiliate-program, market.partnerstack.com/program/freshbooksusa).
  - Everlance on Impact: $2 per app conversion (linkclicky.com/affiliate-program/everlance, a third-party page dated 2024. Confirm before relying on it).
  - Gigaverse affiliate: $10 per paid subscriber + 20% recurring for up to 24 months; approval required (gigaverse.ai/affiliate-program).
  - Amazon Associates (phone mounts, dash cams, insulated delivery bags). Free, but sales are needed to stay approved.
  - Your own digital product (spreadsheet kit) on Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy, which take a fee per sale only.
## Facts used in the tools (check these every year)
- SE tax = 15.3% × 92.35% of net profit, owed at $400+ in net earnings (Jackson Hewitt / IRS).
- 2026 SS wage base $184,500 and standard mileage rate $0.725/mi (from the tnaado page; it also says $0.76 from July 1). **Not checked against irs.gov. Check irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates before launch.**
